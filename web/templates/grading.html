🩺 Card Grading (Computer Vision + local AI)

Analyzes a photo of the physical card to estimate an objective 1-10 grade, PSA/BGS style: Centering, Edges, and Corners are measured deterministically (OpenCV), Surface (scratches/creases) by a local Vision model (Ollama + llava, no data leaves your machine). Card cropping is manual: drag the 4 corners over the uploaded photo.

⚠️ Known limitation: Centering isn't always reliably detected on real photos (a conservative value is used), and the Edges/Corners thresholds haven't been tuned yet on a dataset of cards with known condition. See .CLAUDE/07-grading.md.

📸 Bulk Grading (multiple photos)

Select multiple photos or an entire folder: all cards are cropped first, one at a time (same manual cropping as above) — no analysis starts until you've confirmed the last crop, so you don't have to wait at the screen between one card and the next. Only after that does the analysis run in sequence on all the cropped photos; you can let it run in the background (as long as this browser tab stays open). Every analyzed card ends up in the "📦 Graded cards awaiting linking" list below.

📦 Graded cards awaiting linking

Analyzed cards (single or bulk) stay here until you link them to a card in the collection — they don't touch collection.db before that. If the server is restarted before linking them, they're lost (no disk persistence for this list, see .CLAUDE/07-grading.md).
